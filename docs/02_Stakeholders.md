# Step Into Their Shoes

### Potential buyer

**1. What matters most**  
- Is it worth it? 

**2. What keeps me up at night**  
- Is it complicated?  
- Costs for installation, maintenance, etc.

**3. Key question**  
- “Is it the right lighting solution for us?”

**4. What do we need from them**  
- Purchase decision

___


### Product owner / Management

**1. What matters most**  
\$$$

**2. What keeps me up at night**  
- Sales  
- Competition on the market

**3. Key question**  
- How to differentiate?

**4. What do we need from them**  
- Decisions and budget approval

___


### Regulators, etc.

**1. What matters most**  
- Regulations, laws and safety

**2. What keeps me up at night**  
- Safety  
- Fair competition

**3. Key question**  
- Does the product meet all the necessary regulations?

**4. What do we need from them**  
- Clear requirements and information

___


### Retailers

**1. What matters most**  
- How does it sell?

**2. What keeps me up at night**  
- High return rate  
- Nobody buys it

**3. Key question**  
- Does it sell?

**4. What do we need from them**  
- Purchase decision
